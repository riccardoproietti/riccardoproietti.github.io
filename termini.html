Only the author, on accounts the author owns and directly manages. No other users are admitted and publishing on behalf of third parties is not supported.

6. Personal data

Data handling is described in the Privacy Policy. In short: everything stays on the author's computer, no third-party data is collected, data obtained from YouTube API Services is re-verified or deleted within 30 days, and nothing is shared.
